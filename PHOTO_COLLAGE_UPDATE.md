## Design Decisions

### Photo Collage Layout
- **Scrapbook aesthetic**: Varied sizes and subtle rotations create organic, personal feel
- **Responsive**: 2 columns on mobile, 3 columns on desktop
- **No captions**: Photos speak for themselves, maintaining emotional impact
- **Color harmony**: Warm burgundy/gold tones match website's OLED aesthetic
- **Spacing**: Generous gaps prevent visual clutter

### Navigation Buttons
- **Consistent styling**: All buttons use secondary variant with burgundy borders
- **Clear labeling**: "back page" and "next page" (lowercase, matching site's tone)
- **Placement**: Centered at bottom of each scene
- **Accessibility**: Large touch targets (min-height 52px)
- **No arrows on back button**: Only forward button has arrow indicator

### Animation Timing
- **Photo collage**: 1.2s fade-in with 0.5s delay (after Message 9)
- **Staggered photos**: Each photo appears 0.2s after previous
- **Navigation buttons**: 2.5s delay in Scene 4 (after signature)
- **Smooth transitions**: All animations use custom easing [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]
